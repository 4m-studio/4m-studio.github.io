Privacy policy

AvatarTracker

Last updated 22 September 2026. This policy covers AvatarTracker, a live face-tracking avatar app for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

AvatarTracker does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Face tracking runs entirely on your iPhone; camera frames are processed in memory and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded by us.

What the app accesses

Camera and face tracking

The front camera is used only while an avatar is on screen. ARKit (on TrueDepth devices) or Apple's Vision framework turns each frame into a handful of numbers — head angle, blink, mouth and gaze — which drive the avatar and are then discarded. No face images, face meshes or biometric templates are kept or sent anywhere.

Avatars you create

Characters you build or import, and your calibration settings, are stored only on your device.

Diagnostics

If you opt in, Apple sends us anonymised crash reports through App Store Connect. These contain no personal information and cannot be linked back to you.

Face data is never used for identification, advertising or any purpose other than animating your avatar in the moment.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because your content never reaches our servers, deleting it is entirely in your hands: remove it inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data. Content stored in iCloud can be removed from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

AvatarTracker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

AvatarTracker relies on Apple's own services (iCloud, App Store, and — where applicable — push notifications). Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in AvatarTracker? Email privacy@4mstudio.app and a human will reply.
