Privacy Policy

How every 4M Studio app treats your data — and where to find each app's full policy.

What every app promises

  • Your data stays on your device unless an app clearly says otherwise.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and never link it across apps.
  • No account needed, except where a connection requires one.

Policy for each app

Each app does different things, so each has its own policy that says exactly what it accesses and why.

AvatarTracker

Face tracking runs entirely on device

StoryReel

Photos are analysed and rendered on device

MeetLog

Audio, transcripts and summaries stay on device

QQNest

Fully offline — the game makes no network requests

Wearly

Clothing photos are analysed on device and never uploaded

WorthView

Balances and holdings only — never transactions
