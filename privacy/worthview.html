Privacy policy

WorthView

Last updated 28 September 2026. This policy covers WorthView, a read-only net-worth dashboard for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

WorthView shows what you own and owe — and nothing more. It is read-only by design: it cannot move money, trade securities or pay bills, and it never downloads your transaction history. 4M Studio runs no server for WorthView, so your financial data never reaches us. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your iPhone

Manual entries and history

Homes, vehicles, cash, crypto, valuables, foreign accounts and loans you add by hand, plus one net-worth snapshot per day, are stored only on your iPhone. The last dashboard is cached for offline viewing using iOS Data Protection.

Connection keys

The keys WorthView uses to read your balances are kept in the iPhone Keychain with device-only access. They are never synced to iCloud and never sent to 4M Studio.

App lock

If you turn on Face ID or Touch ID, WorthView asks again after two minutes away, and balances are hidden in the app switcher. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never reaches the app or us.

Account connections

Connecting accounts is optional. You choose any of the following; each one reads balances (and, where available, investment holdings) only. WorthView never asks for or sees your bank username, password or MFA codes.

SimpleFIN Bridge

You connect your institutions on SimpleFIN's own site and give WorthView a setup token. Your iPhone then talks to SimpleFIN directly and requests balances only — no transaction history. SimpleFIN is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy.

Apple Wallet (FinanceKit)

Where available, and only with your permission, WorthView reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings balances through Apple's FinanceKit. This data is read on your iPhone and never leaves it. Transactions are not used.

Personal Plaid relay

If you use Plaid, you sign in to your bank inside Plaid Link, and requests go through a small relay that you deploy on your own Cloudflare account — 4M Studio does not operate it. The relay keeps no database or logs. It seals Plaid's access token so that only the relay can open it, and your iPhone stores that sealed token in the Keychain. Because Plaid does not offer balances on their own for bank accounts, bank connections are created with the smallest possible transaction window (one day); WorthView never requests or reads any transactions.

Balances and prices are reported by your institutions and may be delayed. Holdings are not live quotes. WorthView is not financial advice.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell or rent your data — it never reaches us.
  • We do not embed advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not request transactions, identity, income, or account and routing numbers.
  • We cannot move money, place trades or pay bills — no part of WorthView can.
  • We do not send notifications that contain balances.

Data retention and deletion

Disconnect an institution from its detail page to remove its key from your iPhone; for Plaid connections the relay also asks Plaid to revoke access. Manual entries are removed when you delete them, and everything WorthView keeps on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app. Accounts you linked at SimpleFIN or in Plaid's portal can also be removed there.

Children

WorthView is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

Depending on what you choose to connect: SimpleFIN Bridge; Plaid Inc., described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy; Cloudflare, which hosts a relay you deploy; and Apple services, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in WorthView? Email support.4mstudio@gmail.com and a human will reply.
