CardPick

Which card?
Just ask.

CardPick tells you which of your credit cards earns the most on what you're about to buy. Type “Costco $200” or “Dinner $120” and get the winning card, what it earns and why — worked out on your iPhone. No card numbers, no bank logins, no accounts.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · iOS 17

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01Ask

Type what you're buying.

Start from the purchase, not the card. The Ask tab takes plain text — a store, an amount or both — and its quick picks show which card wins each everyday category before you type a thing.

  • “Costco $200”, “Dinner $120”, “$50 gas” — understood on your iPhone
  • Quick picks for dining, grocery, gas, travel, streaming and more
  • Not sure what you meant? It offers category chips instead of guessing
  • Rotating bonuses read “5% if activated” until you confirm them

02Recommendation

One clear answer.

Search “Uber” and CardPick names the card to use, what it earns and why — then ranks every card in your wallet for the same purchase, so you can see how close the others come.

  • The winning card and its rate, front and centre
  • The reason in plain words — “Transit: taxis, rideshare, parking… earns 3%”
  • Points and miles stay points unless you set your own value
  • Offers you've added stack on top of the card's usual earning

03Benefits

All your benefits, at a glance.

The Benefits tab turns fine print into a dashboard: how many cards you carry, how many bonus categories they cover, and when this quarter's 5% runs out.

  • Best card for every category, with the “if activated” upside beside it
  • Your cards in a swipeable carousel with their headline rates
  • A badge counts bonuses still waiting for activation
  • Spending caps are shown — never guessed

04Wallet

Card products, not card numbers.

Add the cards you carry by product name. CardPick never asks for numbers, logins or account access, and everything it keeps stays on your iPhone.

  • Popular US cards from Chase, Amex, Discover, Capital One, Citi and Wells Fargo
  • Add offers from your card's app — like 10% back at Nike
  • Refresh reads the issuer's public bonus calendar, only when you tap
  • No accounts, no analytics, no AI services

Privacy at a glance

  • Only card products — never numbers, logins or account access
  • Every recommendation is worked out on your iPhone
  • No accounts, no analytics, no AI services
