CardPick

Which card?
Just ask.

CardPick tells you which of your credit cards earns the most on what you're about to buy. Type “Costco $200” or “Dinner $120” and get the winning card, what it earns and why — worked out on your iPhone. No card numbers, no bank logins, no accounts.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

🔎

Just type the purchase

“Costco $200”, “Uber”, “United flight $600” — CardPick recognises the merchant and category, and asks with quick category chips when it isn't sure.

🏆

One clear winner

The best card in your wallet, what it earns on this purchase and why — plus a side-by-side comparison of every card you carry.

🔄

Rotating 5%, handled honestly

Quarterly bonus categories count only after you mark them activated. Until then you'll see “5% if activated” and how many days are left.

📊

Your benefits at a glance

See the best card for each category, bonuses waiting on activation, and when this quarter's 5% ends.

🏷️

Your offers stack on top

Saw 10% back at Nike in your card's app? Add it, and CardPick includes it on top of the card's usual earning.

🎯

No made-up numbers

Points and miles show as points unless you set your own value per point, and spending caps are shown — never guessed.

Privacy at a glance

  • Only card products — never numbers, logins or account access
  • Every recommendation is worked out on your iPhone
  • No accounts, no analytics, no AI services
