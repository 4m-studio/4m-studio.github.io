QQNest

A cosy nest
for Qiao Que.

QQNest is a light companion game built around Qiao Que (桥鹊), a voice-room host. Feed her, decorate her room, play mini-games to earn treats, and watch your bond grow — fully offline, with no account and no purchases.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

🐦

Chibi Qiao Que

Eight expressions and idle animations, all drawn in code — she reacts to everything you do.

🍰

Feed and bond

Treats raise your intimacy level and unlock new stages, outfits and moments together.

🧩

Two mini-games

A 1024 merge puzzle and a match-3 with combos, special pieces and levels — both pay out in treats.

🏠

A room to decorate

Bed, desk, window, sofa and a music corner, with decorations you unlock as you play.

🏆

Collection and daily tasks

Foods, outfits, decorations, expressions and achievements to collect, plus daily tasks to come back for.

📴

Offline, no purchases

No account, no network, no in-app purchases. Your progress lives on your iPhone.

Privacy at a glance

  • Fully offline — the game makes no network requests
  • Progress is stored only on your iPhone
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases
