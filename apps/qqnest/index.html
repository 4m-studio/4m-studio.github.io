QQNest

A cosy nest
for Qiao Que.

QQNest is a little companion game built around Qiao Que (桥鹊), a fox-eared voice-room host. Pat him, feed him treats, play two mini-games to bring gifts home, and watch your bond grow from “First meeting” to “A special giraffe” — fully offline, with no account and no purchases.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · iOS 16

Tap Qiao Que to pat him, or feed him a treat. Your bond is saved in this browser.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01The nest

Qiao Que lives here

His room has a bed, a desk with a lamp, curtains that sway and a record player in the corner. Pat him and he bounces, blushes or grins; feed him and he answers with a line of his own.

  • Eight moods — happy, surprised, shy, sleepy, cuddly, waving, cheering and calm
  • Six treats, from candy to a mystery gift, each with its own reactions
  • Hand-drawn layered art that sways, blinks and twitches its ears

02Games

Play a minute or two, bring something home

In the 1024 merge game, matching gifts climb a ten-step ladder — candy, cookie, strawberry milk, cake… all the way up to a limited-edition Qiao Que. The higher the combo, the bigger the reward.

  • Up to 200+ stars a round, plus treats
  • Daily tasks: feed, pat, play two rounds, merge a 128
  • Best scores and achievements are kept

03Match-3

Pick a basket of strawberries

The match-3 opens with hand-made levels — like 20 strawberries in 20 moves — and then keeps generating new ones. Line up four for an antler rocket, five for a Qiao Que planet, a T or L for a microphone bomb.

  • Shuffle and hammer boosters
  • Cascades and chain reactions
  • Levels that keep coming

04Bond

Four stages of getting close

Every pat and treat adds to your bond, and each stage unlocks something new: a shy face and new greetings, then a rug and a yawn — and finally a limited photo together.

  • Daily tasks that pay out stars and treats
  • A collection of foods, decorations, expressions and achievements
  • Pick your giraffe avatar and nickname
🔊

Sounds made on the fly

No audio files: every pop, sparkle and level-up tune is synthesised live — and it never interrupts the music you're listening to.

🫧

Gentle motion

Respects Reduce Motion: fewer bounces, less movement and fewer particles, everywhere in the game.

📴

Offline, no purchases

No account, no network, no ads, no in-app purchases. Your progress lives on your iPhone.

Privacy at a glance

  • Fully offline — the game makes no network requests
  • Progress is stored only on your iPhone
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases
