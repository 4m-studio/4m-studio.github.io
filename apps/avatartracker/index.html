AvatarTracker

Your face.
Her expressions.

AvatarTracker turns the front camera into a live puppet rig. Blink, talk, smile or tilt your head and your avatar does it with you — then tap to add a wave, a heart or a little dance on top.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · Camera

Tracking you

Move your mouse — she's watching. Click her for a move.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01Where it began

It started with a stick figure.

A circle, two dots and a line: the very first AvatarTracker character. It proved the idea — your blinks, brows, mouth and head tilt, drawn back at you in real time. Stick is still in the app, and still the quickest way to see the tracking at work.

  • Eyes, brows and mouth follow yours live
  • Arms that shoot up for 🎉 Celebrate
  • Clean lines that stay sharp at any size

02A drawing

Then the lines became a drawing.

Mika is drawn in clean shapes and flat colour, then cut into parts — eyes, lids, brows, three mouths, hair in front and behind, and arms with real elbows — so every piece can move on its own while your face drives it.

  • Eyes that blink and follow your gaze
  • Jointed arms: tap 👋 and she waves
  • Five hand shapes, from open palm to peace sign

03Layered 2D

Now she's a fully painted 2D character.

MieMie is painted in full detail and split into 36 layers: lashes, irises and lids, three mouth shapes, hair that swings behind the head, and hands drawn for seven poses. Tap 🫶 and she makes a finger heart while she keeps blinking and talking with you.

  • 36 layers, rigged with a real arm skeleton
  • Seven hand poses, including a finger heart
  • Reactions blend on top of live tracking

04What's next

One cast, one tap — and 3D is next.

Home keeps the whole cast in one row. Pick Stick, Mika or MieMie and Go Live — switching never restarts the camera. Next on the roadmap: 3D avatars, driven by the same face tracking.

  • Switch characters without restarting the camera
  • The same seven reactions on every character
  • Planned next: 3D avatars
🎭

Real-time face tracking

ARKit on TrueDepth iPhones, Apple Vision on every other one — blink, mouth, smile, brows, gaze and head pose.

🎚️

Calm, snappy motion

A quick calibration to your neutral face, then every channel is smoothed on its own: blinks stay crisp, head motion stays calm.

🔒

Frames never leave the phone

Each camera frame becomes a handful of numbers in memory and is thrown away. No face images are stored or uploaded.

Privacy at a glance

  • Face tracking runs entirely on device
  • No face images, meshes or biometric templates are kept
  • No account, no ads, no third-party trackers
