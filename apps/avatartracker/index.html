AvatarTracker

Your face.
Her expressions.

AvatarTracker turns the front camera into a live puppet rig. Blink, talk, smile or tilt your head and your avatar does it with you — then tap to add a wave, a heart or a little dance on top.

iPhone · Camera

Tracking you

Move your mouse — she's watching. Click her for a move.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

🎭

Real-time face tracking

ARKit on TrueDepth iPhones, Apple Vision on every other one — blink, mouth, smile, brows, gaze and head pose.

🧑‍🎨

Layered 2D characters

Hand-drawn characters built from separate layers: eyes that blink, lips that sync, hair that swings and arms with real joints.

👋

Gestures that blend in

Wave, heart, peace, clap, dance… presets sit on top of live tracking, so she keeps blinking and talking mid-gesture.

🎚️

Calm, snappy motion

A quick calibration to your neutral face, then every channel is smoothed on its own: blinks stay crisp, head motion stays calm.

🧍

A growing cast

MieMie, Mika and the classic stick figure today. Switching characters never restarts the camera.

🔒

Frames never leave the phone

Each camera frame becomes a handful of numbers in memory and is thrown away. No face images are stored or uploaded.

Privacy at a glance

  • Face tracking runs entirely on device
  • No face images, meshes or biometric templates are kept
  • No account, no ads, no third-party trackers
