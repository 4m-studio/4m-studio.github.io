Wearly

Your daily outfit,
decided.

Wearly is an AI wardrobe stylist that knows what you own, where you're going and what the weather is doing. Open it in the morning and you already know what to wear — built from clothes already in your closet.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

☀️

Dressed for the day

Outfits planned around the whole day's forecast — a cardigan for a 61° morning that turns into a 76° afternoon.

📅

Knows your plans

Reads your calendar as occasions — team meeting, dinner, gym — and dresses you for all of them at once.

💬

Why this works

Every pick comes with a plain sentence explaining it. No scores, no jargon.

📸

Snap your closet

Photograph your clothes and each piece is cut out and tagged on device — colour, pattern, fabric, warmth, formality.

🧳

Stylist chat and packing

Ask for alternatives in plain words, or get a capsule packing list: the fewest pieces for the most outfits.

🔒

Photos stay home

Garment photos never leave your iPhone. Calendar event titles never leave it either.

Privacy at a glance

  • Clothing photos are analysed on device and never uploaded
  • Calendar events are reduced to an occasion — titles stay on device
  • No ads, no third-party trackers
