LingoPal

Two big buttons.
No language barrier.

LingoPal is a Chinese ↔ English translator made with older parents in mind. Tap 说中文 or Speak English, talk, and read the translation in big type — or let LingoPal say it out loud. Speech and text are translated on your iPhone, so it keeps working in airplane mode, with no account and nothing uploaded.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · iOS 26

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01Speak

Tap a button. Say it. Read it.

Tap 说中文 or Speak English and talk. Your words fill the top card as you speak, and each finished sentence is translated right away underneath — in the largest type that fits, like subtitles. Tap again to stop.

  • The cards show the latest sentences like subtitles; the full text is kept
  • Misheard a word? Tap the text to fix it — only that sentence is translated again
  • Or type and paste; the direction is detected for you
  • Up to five minutes per recording

02Show

Hand it across the table.

One tap fills the screen with the translation in very large type and turns the brightness up. Flip it 180° for the person sitting opposite, switch between black and white, or let LingoPal read it out loud.

  • Read aloud with the voices already on your iPhone
  • The original stays underneath in smaller type
  • The screen stays awake while it's showing
  • If a number or a “not” may have been lost, LingoPal says so

03Phrases

Ready before anything downloads.

56 hand-written travel phrases in 9 categories — basics, airport, directions, restaurant, shopping, transport, hotel, health and emergency. Tap the speaker to say the English, or tap a phrase to show it big.

  • Written by hand, not machine-translated
  • Chinese first, English underneath
  • Covers allergies, feeling unwell and calling for help

04Offline

Works in airplane mode.

Setup is a single button: it asks for the microphone and downloads what's needed, once. After that, translation, speech recognition and the voice all run on the iPhone. Apple Translation is the default; Tencent's open Hy‑MT2 model is an optional download in Advanced.

  • Apple Translation needs no model — iOS fetches the language packs once
  • Hy‑MT2 (1.13 GB, or 1.47 GB high-precision) is often more natural for casual and mixed speech
  • Downloads resume if interrupted and are checked before use
  • An airplane-mode check in Advanced confirms it all works offline
👓

Easy to see, easy to tap

Chinese-first bilingual labels, your system text size, VoiceOver, a high-contrast mode and buttons at least 52 pt tall.

⭐️

History and favorites

Recent translations and the ones you star are one tap away — kept only on the phone and left out of iCloud backup.

Privacy at a glance

  • Speech and text are translated on your iPhone — never uploaded
  • History and favorites stay on the phone, outside iCloud backup
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads
