LingoPal

Two big buttons.
No language barrier.

LingoPal is a Chinese ↔ English translator made with older parents in mind. Tap 说中文 or Speak English, talk, and read the translation in big type — or let LingoPal say it out loud. Speech and text are translated on your iPhone, so it keeps working in airplane mode, with no account and nothing uploaded.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

🎙️

Two big buttons

说中文 on the left, Speak English on the right. Tap once to talk and again to stop — a pulsing frame, a timer and live text show it's listening.

✍️

Check it, then translate

What you said lands in an editable box, so a misheard word can be fixed first. You can also type or paste — the direction is detected for you.

🔊

Big text, read aloud

The translation shows in large type with a big Read aloud button. If a number or a “not” may have been lost, LingoPal says so.

🔄

Show the other person

One tap fills the screen with the translation, turns up the brightness and can flip it 180° for someone across the table.

📖

A phrasebook from day one

56 hand-written travel phrases in 9 categories — airport, directions, restaurant, hotel, health, emergency and more — before anything is downloaded.

✈️

Works in airplane mode

Apple's on-device language packs, or an optional open model you download once. Speech recognition and voices run on the iPhone too, and Setup has an airplane-mode self-test.

👓

Easy to see, easy to tap

Chinese-first bilingual labels, your system text size, VoiceOver, a high-contrast mode and buttons at least 52 pt tall.

⭐️

History and favorites

Recent translations and the ones you star are one tap away — kept only on the phone and left out of iCloud backup.

🧠

Choose your engine

Apple Translation by default, or Tencent's open Hy‑MT2 model running right on the iPhone. Each download is resumable and checked before it's used.

Privacy at a glance

  • Speech and text are translated on your iPhone — never uploaded
  • History and favorites stay on the phone, outside iCloud backup
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads
