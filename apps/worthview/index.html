WorthView

Your wealth,
read only.

WorthView is a privacy-first net-worth dashboard for iPhone. Bring your bank, card and brokerage balances together with the things no bank knows about — your home, your car, your crypto — and watch one honest number over time. It can see balances. It has no way to move money.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · iOS 17

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01Overview

One honest number.

Net worth sits at the top with how far it has moved and a chart you can take from one month to all time. Below it, assets against liabilities in a single bar, then an allocation breakdown of where it all sits.

  • 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y and All — the chart and change badge follow along
  • Debt shown as a share of your assets, not just a total
  • A private daily snapshot on your iPhone builds your trend over time
  • Each currency is totalled separately — no silent exchange-rate guesses

02Assets

Everything you own and owe.

Linked accounts are grouped into cash, investments and retirement, each with its own total. Quick add covers what no bank reports — your home, a car, cash on hand, crypto, valuables, foreign accounts or a loan.

  • Every row says Linked or Manual, so you know where a number came from
  • Manual values untouched for 90 days are flagged for a refresh
  • Investment accounts open to their holdings — institution-reported, not live quotes
  • Manual entries are stored only on your iPhone

03Privacy

Read only, by design.

The Privacy tab spells out what WorthView cannot do: transfer money, trade securities, pay bills, or see your bank passwords and MFA codes. None of it is switched off — it was never built.

  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID, asked again after two minutes away
  • Balances are hidden in the app switcher
  • Connection keys live in the iPhone Keychain and never sync to iCloud
  • Balances only — WorthView never downloads your transaction history
🔗

Connect your way

SimpleFIN Bridge for most US banks, cards and brokerages, Apple Wallet balances read on-device, or a personal Plaid relay you run yourself. Or skip connections and add everything by hand.

🕒

Honest about freshness

Out-of-date values are clearly labelled. If a refresh fails, your last good numbers stay on screen, marked as stale — never silently wiped.

📊

Holdings at a glance

Open a brokerage or retirement account to see each holding's shares, price and value, exactly as your institution reports them.

Privacy at a glance

  • Balances and holdings only — never transactions
  • Bank sign-in happens at SimpleFIN or Plaid, never inside WorthView
  • No 4M Studio server — entries, history and keys stay on your iPhone
