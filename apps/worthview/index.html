WorthView

Your wealth,
read only.

WorthView is a privacy-first net-worth dashboard. Connect your banks and brokerages, add the things they don't know about, and watch one honest number over time. It can see balances — it has no way to move money.

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

🏦

Banks and brokerages

Connect through Plaid Link to see balances and investment holdings in one place.

🙅

Read-only by design

No transfers, trades, bill-pay, account numbers or transaction history. The capability simply isn't built.

🏠

Everything else, by hand

Property, cars, cash, foreign accounts or loans — add them manually; they stay only on your iPhone.

📈

One number over time

Daily net-worth snapshots build a history you can trust, with clear freshness labels on every value.

🛡️

Locked when you're away

Face ID after two minutes away, and values are hidden in the app switcher.

🔌

Disconnect any time

Removing an institution revokes access and deletes its stored token from the server.

Privacy at a glance

  • Balances and holdings only — never transactions
  • Bank credentials go to Plaid, never to us
  • Manual assets and history stay on your iPhone
