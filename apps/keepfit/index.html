KeepFit

Snap your meal.
See your day.

KeepFit puts calories and macros, workouts, steps, water, sleep and weight on one Today screen. Apple Health fills most of it in for you; snap a meal and AI estimates what's on the plate. Trends then shows where your weight is really heading, smoothed into a 7-day average with an ETA to your target.

Coming soon Privacy

iPhone · iOS 17 · Apple Health

What it does

Built for one job,
done properly.

01Today

Your whole day on one screen.

Calories left, with protein, carbs and fat; activity rings for steps, exercise and active energy; meals, workouts, water and sleep, then your latest weigh-in. Swipe the week strip, or pull it down for a month calendar, to see any day. Tap a tile for the full detail.

  • Workout calories can be added to the day's budget
  • Steps, workouts and sleep arrive from Apple Health
  • +8 fl oz of water in one tap

02Journal

Snap a meal. AI logs it.

Take a photo, or just describe what you ate, and AI names each food and estimates its calories, protein, carbs and fat for you to check before saving. Everything lands in the Journal, a timeline of every meal, workout, weigh-in and night's sleep, tagged by where it came from. Each day gets its own card: that day's meal photos, its net calories and a Lose, Even or Gain day badge.

  • Photo or text — “2 eggs, toast, black coffee” works too
  • Filter, search, jump to a date, swipe to edit or delete
  • Health, AI and Imported tags show where each entry came from

03Photos

Every meal, on one photo wall.

Switch the Journal to Photos and every meal you've snapped falls into a two-column waterfall, each photo at its own shape, with its name, calories and day. Prefer rows? The List view is one tap away.

  • Search finds a dish weeks back
  • Tap a photo to open and edit the meal
  • Days, Photos or List — the Journal remembers your pick

04Trends

See past the day-to-day swings.

Weight comes first: every weigh-in as a dot, a 7-day average that smooths out water weight, your target line, your weekly rate and an ETA. Below it, calories, macros, active energy, steps, workouts, sleep, water and resting heart rate over a week, a month or a year.

  • Scrub a chart to read any day, as in Apple Health
  • Long-press a card to reorder it, or hide what you don't track
  • 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M and 1Y ranges

05Your AI, your key

AI on your terms — and your bill.

Pick Claude or Gemini and paste your own API key; it stays in the iPhone's Keychain, and testing it is free. Photos are resized before upload, answers are kept short and the same photo is never analysed twice, so a meal costs a fraction of a cent. Settings shows this month's analyses and the estimated cost.

  • Cheapest capable model by default
  • Photos go straight from your iPhone to the provider you chose
  • No AI key? Log meals by hand — everything else works
🎯

A plan from your profile

Set up asks your height, age, activity and goal — lose, maintain or gain, with a weekly pace — and works out calories, macros, water, steps and exercise for you.

📥

Bring your history

Import a CSV or cells copied from Google Sheets. Columns are recognised in English or Chinese, duplicates are counted before anything is saved, and every import can be undone.

🔒

Yours, on your iPhone

No account and nothing to sign up for. Everything you log stays on the phone, and Apple Health is only read, never written.

Privacy at a glance

  • Everything you log is stored only on your iPhone
  • Apple Health is read-only — KeepFit never writes to it
  • Meal photos go only to the AI provider you pick, with your own key
