AvatarTracker

Your face, her expressions. AvatarTracker turns the front camera into a live puppet rig — blink, talk, smile or tilt your head and your avatar does it with you, while one tap adds a wave, a heart or a little dance on top. Meet MieMie: she's the one watching your cursor.

  • Real-time face tracking with ARKit (TrueDepth) or Vision on any iPhone
  • Layered 2D characters with blinking eyes, lip-sync, bouncing hair and IK arms
  • One-tap gestures and expressions that blend with live tracking — nothing freezes

WorthView

Your wealth, read only. WorthView is a privacy-first net-worth dashboard: connect your banks and brokerages, add the things they don't know about, and watch one honest number over time. It can see balances — it has no way to move money.

  • Balances and investment holdings from your institutions, via Plaid
  • Read-only by design — no transfers, trades, bill-pay or transaction history
  • Manual assets and daily snapshots stay on device, behind Face ID

The studio

Small on purpose.

4M Studio is an independent iOS studio. We don't chase feature lists or funding rounds — we ship a handful of apps, maintain them properly, and answer our own support email.

Everything we make follows the same rules: it should open instantly, work offline, keep your data on your device, and never ask you to watch an ad. If a feature can't be made simple, it doesn't ship.
