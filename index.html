The studio

Small on purpose.

4M Studio is an independent iOS studio. We don't chase feature lists or funding rounds — we ship a handful of apps, maintain them properly, and answer our own support email.

Everything we make follows the same rules: it should open instantly, keep your data on your device, and never ask you to watch an ad. If a feature can't be made simple, it doesn't ship.
