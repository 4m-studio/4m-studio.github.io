Last updated 27 September 2026. This policy covers CardPick, an iPhone app from 4M Studio that suggests which of your credit cards to use for a purchase.

The short version

CardPick works entirely on your iPhone. It only needs to know which card products you carry — never card numbers, bank logins or account access. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no AI services, and nothing you type is sent to us.

What the app stores

Your cards and settings

The card products you choose (for example “Freedom Flex”), which bonus categories you've marked as activated, and any cents-per-point values you enter are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption.

Offers you add

Offers you type in yourself (such as “10% back at Nike”) are stored the same way, only on your iPhone.

What you search for

Purchases you type, like “Costco $200”, are interpreted on the device to make a recommendation. Recent searches are kept only while the app is open and are never uploaded.

Refreshing card benefits

Card reward rates ship inside the app. When you tap Refresh in Wallet, CardPick may download the issuer's public rewards calendar for cards that support it (currently Discover it Cash Back, from discover.com). These requests carry no cookies, no sign-in and nothing about you or your cards; as with any web request, the issuer's server sees your IP address. Nothing is fetched unless you tap Refresh.

Recommendations are estimates based on published card terms. Your issuer decides what you actually earn, and CardPick can't see your transactions or how much of a spending cap you've used. CardPick is not financial advice.

What we never do

  • We never ask for card numbers, security codes, bank logins or account access.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your data — it never reaches us.
  • We do not embed advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not send your searches to AI or other online services.

Data retention and deletion

Remove a card or an offer in the app to delete it. Deleting the app removes everything CardPick has stored on your iPhone.

Children

CardPick is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

CardPick has no third-party SDKs. Benefit refreshes contact the card issuer's public website directly. The app relies on Apple services, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Card names are trademarks of their issuers; CardPick is not affiliated with or endorsed by any issuer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact
