Privacy policy

MeetLog

Last updated 19 August 2026. This policy covers MeetLog, a meeting recording and notes app for iPhone and iPad published by 4M Studio.

The short version

MeetLog does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There are no ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics SDKs. Your content stays on your device.

What the app accesses

Audio recordings

Recordings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. We have no access to them. Transcription and summarisation run on device.

Microphone access

Used only while you are actively recording. MeetLog never records in the background without a visible indicator.

Calendar access (optional)

If you grant it, MeetLog reads event titles and times so it can name recordings. Calendar data stays on your device.

Diagnostics

If you opt in, Apple sends us anonymised crash reports through App Store Connect. These contain no personal information and cannot be linked back to you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because your content never reaches our servers, deleting it is entirely in your hands: remove it inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data.

Children

MeetLog is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

MeetLog relies on Apple's own services (App Store, and — where applicable — push notifications). Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in MeetLog? Email privacy@4mstudio.app and a human will reply.
