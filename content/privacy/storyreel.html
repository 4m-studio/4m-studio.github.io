Last updated 23 September 2026. This policy covers StoryReel, a travel memory video app for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

StoryReel does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There are no ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics SDKs. Your content stays on your device.

What the app accesses

Photos and their metadata

StoryReel reads the photos you choose, including when and where they were taken, so it can group them into chapters and animate your route. Photo analysis and video rendering happen entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us.

Speech recognition (optional)

If you dictate a caption, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text. StoryReel does not record or keep voice audio.

AI captions (optional, off by default)

If you add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings and ask for a suggestion, StoryReel sends the selected photos' content together with place and date context to Google to draft titles or captions. This is the only network request the app makes, it happens only when you ask, and it is governed by Google's terms. Without a key, nothing is sent.

Music

Built-in tracks ship with the app. If you choose your own audio file, it is read on device only while rendering.

Diagnostics

If you opt in, Apple sends us anonymised crash reports through App Store Connect. These contain no personal information and cannot be linked back to you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because your content never reaches our servers, deleting it is entirely in your hands: remove it inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data.

Children

StoryReel is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

StoryReel relies on Apple's own services (App Store, and — where applicable — push notifications). Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in StoryReel? Email support.4mstudio@gmail.com and a human will reply.
