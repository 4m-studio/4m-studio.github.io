Last updated 4 October 2026. This policy covers LingoPal (随译), an iPhone app from 4M Studio that translates between Chinese and English, by text or by voice.

The short version

LingoPal translates on your iPhone. What you say and type is turned into text and translated on the device, and is never sent to us or to any online translation or AI service. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Microphone and speech recognition

LingoPal asks for the microphone and speech recognition only so it can hear what you say after you tap 说中文 or Speak English. Audio goes straight from the microphone to Apple's on-device speech recognizer. It is never saved to a file and never uploaded.

What the app stores

History and favorites

Recent translations and the ones you mark as favorites are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption and excluded from iCloud and computer backups. You can delete them one by one or all at once.

Settings

Your choices, such as the translation engine, speaking speed and high-contrast mode, are kept on your iPhone.

Logs and diagnostics

The app's system logs record only timings, sizes and status codes, never your words or audio. The Diagnostics screen measures speed and memory on your iPhone; its report leaves the phone only if you choose to share it yourself.

Downloads

By default LingoPal uses Apple Translation. Its Chinese and English language packs, the speech recognition models and the voices are downloaded and managed by iOS through Apple's own prompts and Settings, under Apple's privacy policy.

If you choose one of the optional Hy‑MT2 translation models in Setup, LingoPal downloads that file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), or from the hf-mirror.com mirror if you turn that option on. Downloads happen only when you tap Download or Update, use Wi‑Fi unless you allow cellular, and carry no account, cookies or information about you; as with any web request, the server sees your IP address. Every file is checked against a fixed SHA‑256 fingerprint before it is used. Apart from these downloads, LingoPal does not connect to the internet.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your data. It never reaches us.
  • We do not send your speech or text to online translation, speech or AI services.
  • We do not embed advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Clear your history and favorites in the app, and delete downloaded models in Setup. Deleting the app removes everything LingoPal has stored on your iPhone. Language packs and voices managed by iOS are removed in the iPhone's Settings.

Children

LingoPal is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

LingoPal has no third-party SDKs. It uses Apple's Translation, Speech and voice frameworks, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. The optional Hy‑MT2 models are published by Tencent under the Apache 2.0 license and run on your iPhone with llama.cpp (MIT license).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
