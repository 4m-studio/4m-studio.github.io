Last updated 22 September 2026. This policy covers WorthView, a read-only net-worth dashboard for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

WorthView shows what you own and owe — and nothing more. It is read-only by design: it cannot move money, place trades or pay bills, and it never requests your transaction history. We do not sell or share your data and there are no ads or trackers.

What the app accesses

Connected institutions (Plaid)

When you connect a bank or brokerage, you sign in through Plaid Link; WorthView never sees your banking credentials. We request only account balances and, where available, investment holdings. We do not request transactions, identity, income, or account-and-routing numbers.

Access tokens

Plaid returns an access token that is stored encrypted on the WorthView server and is never sent back to the app. Disconnecting an institution revokes the connection and deletes its token.

Manual assets and history

Property, cars, cash or liabilities you add by hand, and your daily net-worth snapshots, are stored only on your iPhone.

App lock

After two minutes away, Face ID or Touch ID is required, and values are hidden in the app switcher. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never reaches us.

Balances and prices are institution-reported and may be delayed. WorthView is not financial advice.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not request, store or analyse your transactions.

Data retention and deletion

Disconnect an institution from its detail page to revoke access and delete its stored token from our server. Manual assets and snapshots are removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Children

WorthView is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

Institution connections are provided by Plaid Inc., whose handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. WorthView also relies on Apple services, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in WorthView? Email privacy@4mstudio.app and a human will reply.
