Last updated 23 September 2026. This policy covers QQNest, a casual companion game for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

QQNest does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There are no ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics SDKs. Your content stays on your device.

What the app accesses

Game progress

Your nickname, intimacy level, collection, achievements and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network

QQNest works fully offline and makes no network requests. There are no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Diagnostics

If you opt in, Apple sends us anonymised crash reports through App Store Connect. These contain no personal information and cannot be linked back to you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because your progress never leaves your device, deleting it is entirely in your hands: reset it in Settings inside the game, or delete the app.

Children

QQNest is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

QQNest relies on Apple's own services (App Store, and — where applicable — push notifications). Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in QQNest? Email support.4mstudio@gmail.com and a human will reply.
