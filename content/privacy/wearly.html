Last updated 22 September 2026. This policy covers Wearly, an AI wardrobe stylist for iPhone published by 4M Studio.

The short version

Wearly does not sell or share your personal data, has no ads and no third-party trackers. Your closet, photos and outfit history stay on your device.

What the app accesses

Photos of your clothes

Photos you add are analysed on device (Apple Vision) to cut out each garment and describe its colour, pattern, fabric, category and warmth. Photos are never uploaded.

Calendar (optional)

If you grant access, Wearly reads upcoming events and reduces each one to an occasion and a formality level, so it can dress you for the day. Event titles never leave your device.

Location and weather (optional)

A one-shot, approximate location is used to fetch the forecast through Apple WeatherKit. Without it, Wearly uses a seasonal estimate instead.

AI stylist

The stylist runs on device. If a hosted model is enabled in a future version, only garment attributes and the classified occasion are sent — never photos, event titles or your location.

Notifications (optional)

Used only for the optional evening outfit reminder.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or link data across apps.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because your content never reaches our servers, deleting it is entirely in your hands: remove it inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data.

Children

Wearly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

Wearly relies on Apple's own services (WeatherKit, App Store, and — where applicable — notifications). Apple's handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Wearly? Email support.4mstudio@gmail.com and a human will reply.
