Last updated 19 October 2026. This policy covers KeepFit (轻记), an iPhone app from 4M Studio for tracking meals, workouts, sleep, water, steps and weight.

The short version

Everything you log in KeepFit is stored on your iPhone. 4M Studio runs no server and never receives your data. KeepFit reads from Apple Health but never writes to it. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you ask AI to analyse a meal: then the photo or description goes directly from your iPhone to the AI provider you chose (Anthropic or Google), using your own API key. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the app stores

Your log

Meals (including any photo you attach), workouts, weigh-ins, sleep, water, steps and imports are saved in the app's database on your iPhone. Like other app data, they are included in your own iPhone backups. KeepFit does not sync them to iCloud or anywhere else.

Your profile and goals

The height, age, sex, weight, activity level and goal you enter during set-up are kept on your iPhone and used only to work out your calorie, macro, water, step and exercise goals.

Settings and AI usage counter

Your settings, the layout of the Trends page and this month's AI usage counts (analyses, tokens and an estimated cost) are kept on your iPhone. Saved AI results, which stop the same photo being analysed twice, are kept in the app's cache on your iPhone.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, KeepFit asks to read steps, active energy, exercise minutes, walking, running and cycling distance, resting heart rate, workouts, sleep, weight, body fat, water, height, biological sex and date of birth. It uses them to fill in your days, your trends and your set-up. KeepFit asks for no permission to write, so it never changes your Health data. Health data is never used for advertising and is never sent to us or to any AI provider. You can change access at any time in Health → Profile → Apps → KeepFit.

Camera and photos

KeepFit uses the camera, or a photo you pick from your library, only when you log a meal. The photo is saved with that meal on your iPhone.

AI meal analysis

AI analysis is optional and off until you add an API key in Settings → AI food analysis. You choose the provider, Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google), and paste your own key, which is stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone.

When you tap Analyze with AI, KeepFit sends that meal's photo, resized and compressed, together with any note you typed, directly from your iPhone to the provider, under your key. With Describe it, only your text is sent. Nothing else is included: not your profile, your other entries or your Health data. The provider handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy for API customers, and bills your account. Tapping Test checks the key against the provider's free list-of-models endpoint and sends no meal data. If you never add a key, KeepFit makes no network requests at all, apart from the link that opens the provider's website to get a key.

  • Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

Importing data

CSV files and cells you paste are read on your iPhone. Each import is recorded so you can undo it from Settings → Import history.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your data. It never reaches us.
  • We do not write to or share your Apple Health data.
  • We do not embed advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Swipe to delete any entry in the Journal, undo an import from Settings, and remove your API key from Settings. Deleting the app removes everything KeepFit has stored on your iPhone. Anything an AI provider received is kept according to that provider's policy for your account.

Health and medical information

Calorie and nutrition figures in KeepFit are estimates and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.

Children

KeepFit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Third-party services

KeepFit has no third-party SDKs. It uses Apple's HealthKit, SwiftData and camera frameworks, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, and, only if you add a key, the Anthropic or Google API described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
